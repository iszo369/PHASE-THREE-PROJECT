# Customer Churn Classification Project

## Overview
This project applies machine learning classification techniques to predict customer churn. The goal is to help business stakeholders identify customers at high risk of leaving and take proactive retention actions.

## Business and Data Understanding
Customer churn directly impacts revenue and long-term business sustainability. The dataset used contains customer demographics, service usage, billing information, and churn labels. The stakeholder audience includes business managers and retention teams who benefit from early churn detection.

## Modeling
A logistic regression model was used as a baseline due to its interpretability and suitability for binary classification. The model was then improved through hyperparameter tuning. This iterative approach allowed performance gains while maintaining transparency.

## Evaluation
Recall was selected as the primary evaluation metric because missing a potential churner is more costly than falsely flagging a loyal customer. Model performance was evaluated on both training and testing data to ensure generalization.

## Conclusion
The final model successfully identifies key drivers of churn and provides actionable insights for retention strategies. While limitations exist, the model offers a strong foundation for data-driven customer retention efforts.
